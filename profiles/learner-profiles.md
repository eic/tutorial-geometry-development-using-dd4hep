---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets ePIC collaboration members — students, postdocs, and researchers — who need to
work with the detector geometry: reading the compact XML description, viewing the geometry, and
modifying or adding subsystems by editing or writing DD4hep detector plugins. Learners are assumed
to have a working `eic-shell` and basic familiarity with the Unix shell, Git, and C++; no prior
experience with DD4hep or ROOT geometry is required.
